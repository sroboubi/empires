# Audio attribution — assets/audio/

All files in this directory are **CC0 (public domain)**. No attribution is
legally required; sources are recorded here for provenance.

Game-ready MP3s (mono, 44.1 kHz) were transcoded/trimmed from these sources
with ffmpeg. `unit_destroy.mp3` is pitch-shifted; `building_destroy.mp3` is a
mix of two sources.
